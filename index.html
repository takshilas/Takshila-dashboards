    Method and data notes

    • Source: client_catalog.marketshare_cubes.ms_estimations_metadata, Britax US instance 1224, one row per ASIN per week (Sunday week start), Amazon US. Window is computed from the latest week in the data ().
    • Price is implied, not a shelf price. ASP = estimated sales ÷ estimated units. It blends promotions, coupons, mix and 1P/3P sellers, so it will not match the price on the detail page on any given day.
    • Competitor brands are every brand flagged as a competitor in the Market Share category (all sellers). Segment-level ASP is volume-weighted, so a competitor with cheaper or more premium models changes the average. Use the SKU table for like-for-like checks.
    • Estimates vs actuals: for Britax, estimated sales for the window are about 77% of Vendor Central ordered revenue ($27.7M vs $35.7M) and estimated ASP is about 8% lower ($234 vs $253). Use this view for relative price position, not for revenue reporting.
    • Double strollers: Britax has no sales in this segment in the window, so only competitor prices are shown.
    • Event weeks: competitor units jump in the weeks starting 2026-06-21 and 2026-10-04, which likely line up with Prime events. Price dips in those weeks are mostly deal pricing. Verify the event dates before quoting them.
    • Last 13 weeks vs prior 13 weeks compares weeks 40–52 with weeks 27–39 of the window.
